# RedVault Messages – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 6 October 2026

Last Updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the RedVault Messages encrypted messaging service (“RedVault Messages”, the “Service”).

It does not govern RedVault Systems' separate cloud storage or backup storage services. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

RedVault Messages is operated by RedVault Systems, based in New Zealand (“RedVault”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

Contact: cybermarkagency@gmail.com

## 1. Our Privacy Approach

RedVault Messages is designed to minimize the information required to communicate privately.

The Service is designed around three principles:

1. You can create a messaging identity using a username without supplying an email address or telephone number.
2. Messages are encrypted on the user's device before transmission.
3. RedVault's servers are designed to carry encrypted message data without needing access to plaintext message contents.

End-to-end encryption protects message content, but it does not mean that no information whatsoever is processed when you use an internet-based communication service.

This Policy explains the difference.

## 2. Information You Provide

### Username

RedVault Messages requires a username so other users can communicate with you.

Your username may be visible to people with whom you communicate.

We do not require an email address or telephone number to create a standard RedVault Messages account unless that practice changes and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly.

### Payment Information

If you purchase a RedVault Messages subscription, payment information may be processed by our payment provider.

Depending on the payment method, this may include:

- name;
- billing address;
- payment method;
- transaction identifier;
- payment status;
- subscription information; and
- information required for fraud prevention and tax compliance.

RedVault should not receive your complete payment-card credentials when those credentials are processed directly by an independent payment provider.

### Support Information

If you contact support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as:

- your name;
- email address;
- username;
- support request;
- screenshots;
- diagnostic information; or
- other information included in your message.

Support communications are separate from end-to-end encrypted conversations inside RedVault Messages unless expressly stated otherwise.

### Abuse Reports

If you submit an abuse or safety report, we may receive:

- your username;
- the reported username;
- the reason for the report;
- screenshots;
- message excerpts voluntarily submitted by you;
- supporting files; and
- information required to investigate the report.

Information intentionally submitted in an abuse report can therefore become readable by RedVault even where the original conversation was end-to-end encrypted.

## 3. Encrypted Message Content

RedVault Messages is designed so that message content is encrypted on the sender's device and decrypted on the intended recipient's device.

RedVault's infrastructure may handle or store encrypted ciphertext necessary to deliver messages.

We are not intended to hold the encryption material necessary to routinely read users' plaintext messages.

Because users control their devices, a recipient can still:

- read a message;
- copy it;
- screenshot it;
- photograph it;
- record it;
- forward it; or
- voluntarily disclose it.

End-to-end encryption does not prevent an intended recipient from sharing a message after receiving it.

## 4. Message Metadata

Providing an encrypted messaging service requires some information that is separate from the contents of messages.

Depending on the technical operation of the Service, this may include information such as:

- your username;
- destination username;
- message identifier;
- encrypted message size;
- sending or delivery timestamp;
- delivery status;
- session information;
- anti-abuse information; and
- information necessary to route encrypted communications.

We attempt to limit metadata to what is reasonably necessary for operation, security, legal compliance and abuse prevention.

## 5. IP Addresses and Connection Information

When your device connects to RedVault infrastructure, technical network information is necessarily transmitted.

This can include:

- IP address;
- date and time of connection;
- network request information;
- application or browser information;
- operating-system information; and
- security-related connection information.

An IP address can potentially reveal an approximate geographic area or identify an internet connection.

RedVault Messages therefore does not promise complete network anonymity.

Network information may be processed where reasonably necessary to:

- establish connections;
- secure the Service;
- investigate attacks;
- prevent abuse;
- troubleshoot failures; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Device and Technical Information

Where necessary to provide and secure the Service, RedVault may process limited technical information concerning the device or software connecting to RedVault Messages, such as:

- application version;
- operating-system version;
- device type;
- crash information;
- security events;
- authentication events; and
- performance or error information.

We do not use technical diagnostics as a reason to inspect plaintext encrypted messages.

## 14. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

- maintaining subscriptions;
- resolving support requests;
- preventing fraud and abuse;
- maintaining security;
- satisfying accounting and tax requirements;
- complying with legal obligations; and
- establishing or defending legal claims.

- financial records required by law;
- information subject to lawful preservation obligations;
- security records required to investigate abuse; or

- access;

- misuse.

The presence of end-to-end encryption may reduce the consequences of certain breaches but does not eliminate RedVault's legal obligations concerning other personal information.
